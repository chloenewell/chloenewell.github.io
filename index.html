About

Learning experience designer with over a decade of direct classroom instruction across ELA and math, plus certified small-group reading intervention, grades 3–6, paired with hands-on experience architecting digital content systems inside a large-scale LMS and an enterprise software environment. I design with learning progressions, misconception-based scaffolding, and cognitive load principles — then build those into interactive, product-ready experiences, using AI-integrated workflows to produce at scale without losing instructional quality.

Across my career I've taught and designed curriculum spanning multiple subject areas, and the underlying systems — gradual release, misconception-based branching, scaffolded assessment — hold constant regardless of subject. I design as readily for corporate instructional design and data-driven learning environments as I do for K-6 classrooms.

Trench Run

Adaptive 3D gameplay mechanics and state-machine scaffolding. A 20-minute, independent Grade 4 digital learning module mapped to Cause & Effect (CCSS.RI.4.3), built as a full 3D cyberpunk game world.

3 mini-games, 1 reusable engine
  • Grounded in Science of Reading research — explicit strategy modeling, systematic scaffolding, and misconception-based branching drive every checkpoint
  • I DO / WE DO / YOU DO gradual release, with a four-tier fail-safe scaffolding ladder that fades support as competence is established
  • Mini-games (Chain Reaction Run, Driftline Chase, Grid Overload) mechanically embody the comprehension skill itself, not quizzes layered onto gameplay
  • Built as a repeatable production system — subject-agnostic state-machine logic and checkpoint structure, engineered to ingest new passages or standards without rebuilding the engine
